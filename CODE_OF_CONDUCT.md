# Code of conduct

Everyone is welcome to participate, regardless of background, identity, or level of technical experience.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people respectfully. Critique ideas, code, and measurement methods without personal attacks.
- Make technical claims with supporting evidence and acknowledge uncertainty. Questions, corrections, and disagreement are welcome.
- Respect privacy. Do not share another person's private information or private conversations without permission.
- Keep discussions relevant and constructive. Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual harassment, and spam are not acceptable.

## Scope and moderation

These rules apply in this repository's issues, pull requests, and other project spaces managed by the maintainer. The maintainer, [pippopapera](https://github.com/pippopapera), may ask participants to change their behavior, edit or remove inappropriate content, lock discussions, or restrict participation. The response will depend on the severity and repetition of the behavior.

For a routine public moderation concern, mention the maintainer in the relevant discussion without repeating private information or abusive content. For harassment, threats, or concerns that should not be reported publicly, use [GitHub's abuse reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).

For software vulnerabilities, follow the separate [security reporting guidance](docs/SECURITY.md#reporting).
